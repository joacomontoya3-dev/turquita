How to cure a new mate

Only natural calabaza and wood mates need curing. Steel, glass and resin are ready to use out of the box.

  1. Fill the gourd with used yerba and top it up with 70–80 °C water.
  2. Leave it 24 hours, topping up the water as it soaks in.
  3. Tip it out and scrape the soft inner pulp away with a teaspoon.
  4. Rinse, then dry it mouth-down out of direct sun for two days.

Never use soap, and never put it in the dishwasher.

How to mate.

Mate is not tea. You refill the same gourd ten or fifteen times, and you pass it around.

The five unwritten rules

Etiquette
  • El cebadorOne person pours, for everyone

    The cebador fills, pours and passes — always back to themselves first, never around the circle hand to hand.

  • Mate del zonzoThe first one is the fool's mate

    It's the bitterest and the weakest. The cebador drinks it themselves. That's the job.

  • No la muevasNever touch the bombilla

    Moving it collapses the montañita and clogs the filter. This is the one that actually annoys people.

  • El ruidoDrink it all — the gurgle is the point

    Slurping the last of it isn't rude, it's the signal you're done. Handing back a half-full mate is.

  • Gracias"Gracias" means you're out

    Say it only when you've had enough. Say it early and the gourd stops coming to you — which is how most first-timers accidentally leave the round.

Curing

A new calabaza isn't ready yet.

Natural gourds have a soft inner pulp that has to come out before you drink from them, or your mate will taste like wet cardboard for a month. It takes 24 hours and a teaspoon.

The scrim

Non-negotiable

Text over video — always

Legible at every frame of the loop.

The single worst defect on the current site is olive text sitting directly on bright footage. Every text-over-video block here uses one shared gradient — transparent at the top, rgba(10,12,7,.92) at the bottom — and the text always sits in the dark end. If a headline needs to move up the frame, the scrim moves with it. In Wix Studio this is a colour overlay on the Video Box set to that gradient, not an opacity slider on the video itself.
